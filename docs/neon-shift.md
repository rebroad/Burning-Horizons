# Explorable Neon Shift

The violet Warehouse doorway leads to explorable city streets. Eighty permanent 10×10×3 m building ground floors stand on an 18 m grid, leaving 8 m streets. Towers reshape every 60 seconds above those bases through Rain City, Circuit Arena and Arcade Sky layouts. Streets, the central arrival area and Warehouse return gate stay fixed; players are never relocated.

`NeonShiftLots.js` is the authoritative lot definition for source export, rendering, the movement guard and publication validation. Tower jitter is at most 0.6 m, width/depth at most 8 m, and moving geometry starts at y=3.05 m. Bodies, glowing edges and window offsets remain over 0.35 m inside every fixed footprint throughout interpolation. Opaque instanced ground floors and 320 lit street panels replace source visuals without changing colliders.

Terrain spans 220 m. The street floor and outer boundary have inner limits ±96 m; the former ±24 m fence is removed. Ten-metre outer walls and a pinned footprint guard block perimeter exits and building reservations, including swept moves and post-collision pushes. Height queries and jump/gravity remain intact. Invalid prior/arrival poses and non-walk modes fail closed. Actual jump feet peak is about 1.13 m, with conservative body top below 2.93 m; there are no raised walkable props forming steps onto a 3 m base.

Return remains `[0,1.8,18]`, spawn `[0,0,14]`. All 320 building sides connect to that area through 121 street nodes and 220 edges. Follow the fixed Warehouse landmark home. Building interiors and rooftop traversal are outside this walking layout.

## Rendering and release

The engine adapter requires the exact world identity and current `ShiftingWorldProfile.js` digest. World data contains no executable behavior. Original scene/art use MIT with no external assets or endpoints. Qualification is the public WebGL game.

One wall-clock state per frame serves previews and the active world. Entry does not reset it. Forward clock jumps catch up directly; rollback freezes until time catches up. Morphs last 2.4 seconds. The fixed pool has 2,458 instances, 6 geometries, 8 materials, 2 textures, 25 mesh objects and 33,502 triangles before renderer-specific passes. This is a new baseline, not unchanged rendering cost. Shifts preserve resource identities and shader structure. Public views stay capped at three; owner/default/native limits remain two, with existing culling and leases.

COMPLETION/NO-PROGRESS, wall movement: architect reproduced a native 0.3 m collision push that violated the 0.35 m footprint guard and stopped diagonal sliding. The final pinned-instance resolver uses 0.4 m as a deliberate local margin, not a universal proof. If a full resolved segment is invalid, only a nonzero axis projection passing the identical full footprint and analytic swept check may preserve sliding; otherwise X/Z roll back and the controller clears horizontal velocity. Pure inward pressure stops. Vertical motion remains intact. The actual resolved segments, including valid prior clearances between 0.35 and 0.4 m, are tested: 145,600 corner probes, 9,100 prior-clearance cases, and positive actual-player movement at 20/30/60 Hz.
